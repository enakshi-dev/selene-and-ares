OUR SHARED RESPONSIBILITY

Explore.
Document.
Protect.

Space heritage is a record of how humanity learned to reach beyond Earth. Preserving it helps future explorers understand where we began.

01

Explore with curiosity.

Artifacts preserve the history of human exploration. They show the challenges, creativity and teamwork behind every mission.

02

Document with care.

Historic sites and instruments can help scientists study how materials and technology change in the space environment.

03

Protect what remains.

Landing sites have scientific and cultural value. Future visitors should observe, record and avoid disturbing historic objects.
